My story

Why I started AMURU.

I moved to Europe when things were hard back home. Far from family, I was learning to look after my mental health and understand my ADHD.

Through yoga, training, writing and my own experiences, I found that real wellbeing begins when we learn to listen to ourselves. Now I’m coming home to Honduras to share what helped me grow.

“I don’t believe in a perfect life. I believe in living with intention.”
Mafer
